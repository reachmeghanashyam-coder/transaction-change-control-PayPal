> But here, PayPal hasn't paid them yet!

### ACT 3: What is TCC? & Fair Settlement (0:42 – 1:05)
* **Visual:** Click **"03 $25 Offer"**. Then click the green button: **"ACCEPT $25 REFUND & CAPTURE $110"**. Center vault turns emerald green: `PARTIAL CAPTURE SETTLED: $110.00` and `$25.00 AUTO-RELEASED`.
* **Voiceover:**
> *"This is Transaction Change Control: an autonomous financial shield that governs the gap between checkout and delivery.  
> Instead of an angry dispute, the buyer stays in control.  
> They can void the order on PayPal with one click and zero fees.  
> Or, accept a fair discount.  
> On the backend, PayPal releases $110 to the store, and automatically returns the remaining $25 back to the buyer's balance.  
> The merchant saves the sale, the buyer gets fair compensation, and PayPal eliminates the dispute."*
